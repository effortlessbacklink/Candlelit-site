CANDLELIT

About

CANDLELIT creates beautiful candlelight installations for weddings, galas, corporate events, private dinners, and special celebrations. From elegant table settings to full-room installations, the team helps create a warm and memorable atmosphere for every occasion.

From design and delivery to setup, styling, candle management, and removal, CANDLELIT takes care of every detail. Visit CANDLELIT to explore past installations, learn more about the consultation process, and find candlelight pieces for your own event.

FAQ

What types of events does CANDLELIT serve?

CANDLELIT works with weddings, galas, corporate and branded events, private dinners, and other elevated celebrations.

Do you offer flameless candle options?

Yes, CANDLELIT offers real-flame, flameless, and enclosed candlelight options to suit different venues and safety requirements.

Does CANDLELIT handle setup and removal?

Yes, the team handles delivery, setup, on-site styling, candle management when needed, and removal after the event.
